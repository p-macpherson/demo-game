# Audio Style

> Light woodwinds over plucked strings, and small, tactile sounds for everything the goblin does: cosy and a little comic.

Written on 2026-11-21 from what was already in the game and its old Overview; Pat hasn't reviewed it as a doc yet, hence draft.

## Mood
Cosy and playful, never tense.

## Music
Small acoustic ensemble: flute and clarinet over pizzicato strings, glockenspiel for rewards. The main loop is in
D dorian at 104 bpm, about a minute long; finding treasure plays a short flourish ending on D major.

## Sound effects
Tactile and close: footsteps, the creak of a chest lid, gold, a paper rustle for the map, quiet forest ambience.
Every action the player takes makes a sound (see [Design Pillars](Design%20Pillars.md)).

## Voice
None: the game has no speech.
