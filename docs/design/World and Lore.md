# World and Lore

> A mossy forest clearing, lit by lanterns, where a small goblin hunts for treasure chests. Cosy and a little comic;
> nothing here is dangerous.

Written on 2026-11-21 from what was already in the game and its old Overview; Pat hasn't reviewed it as a doc yet, hence draft.

## Setting
One forest clearing: grass, a winding cobblestone path, a pond, barrels and mossy boulders as landmarks, lanterns.

## Tone
Cosy, light and playful. No threat, no darkness.

## Characters
The goblin: small (1.1 m), curious, delighted by gold. Nobody else lives here yet.
