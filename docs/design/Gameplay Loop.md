# Gameplay Loop

> Explore the clearing, open every chest, win. Walking, one interact key, and a camera on the mouse.

Written on 2026-11-21 from what was already in the game and its old Overview; Pat hasn't reviewed it as a doc yet, hence draft.

## Core loop
0. The game opens on the title screen over the clearing: **Play** starts, **Quit** exits.
1. Explore the clearing.
2. Near a closed chest, press **E** to open it: the lid swings open, gold is added to the counter and the goblin waves.
3. A treasure map lies in the clearing: press **E** next to it to pick it up (a paper rustle) and the HUD shows "Map found".
4. When every chest is open, the clearing is cleared: the win screen shows the gold total and **Play again** resets the clearing.

## Controls and camera
| Input | Does |
|---|---|
| **WASD** | Walk |
| Right mouse button + drag | Turn the camera |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom |
| **E** | Open a chest, pick up the map |
| **Esc** | Pause (Resume, master volume, Quit to title) |

The HUD shows gold, chests opened out of the total, "Map found", and the E prompt near a chest or the map.

## Challenge
None: there is no way to fail. The only goal is finding every chest.
